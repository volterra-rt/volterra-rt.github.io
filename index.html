(01)About us———

Wo we are

We are a robotics team from Liceo Scientifico Vito Volterra: we apply theoretical knowledge to the practical development of projects, taking care of both hardware and software.

Our mission is to turn a passion for robotics into concrete, competitive solutions, tackling complex challenges with technical rigour.

We were founded in the summer of 2024 and immediately achieved a significant result: winning the “Project Quality” award at the 2025 Cesena National Finals.

Volterra
Remote Farmer

Arduino · ESP32 App & cloud Cesena 2025 winner

A robotic device that measures the health of a plant or the soil: moisture and light. It sends an image to the app and lets you water the plant remotely, manually or automatically.

A smart system for plant care and monitoring, with remote control and a live feed: irrigation and data you can read from anywhere in the world.

“Project Quality” Award — Cesena National Finals 2025

Volterra
Blue Sentinels

Autonomous buoy Solar Genoa · Maker Faire Rome 2025

A buoy that measures water and atmospheric parameters: turbidity and dissolved solids, wave height, temperature, pressure and wind speed. The data are sent to a website with a chart for each parameter.

Real-time environmental monitoring (every 10 seconds), fully autonomous thanks to solar panels.

Presented in Genoa and at Maker Faire Rome 2025

Volterra
Art Sentinel

Raspberry Pi & ESP32 Cloud and remote control WRO Italy 2026 third place

A smart display case capable of monitoring and acting on your artworks. Completely modular, allowing to connect up to 127 external modules on-the-go, thanks to our custom-made PCB, that allows to have full control on the system keeping cost and size low.

Remote real-time monitoring, accessing the data in real time. The OpenAPI-compatible API allows to manage multiple devices at the same time for different users.

Third place in WRO Italy 2026, Ninht place in WRO Open India 2026, Hyderabad
